## Welcome to Personal Academic Pages
### About Me

![Me.jpg](https://github.com/Shen-Xiaoxiao/Shen-Xiaoxiao.github.io/blob/main/images/Me.jpg)

I am currently an Associate Professor (Special Appointment) in the Department of Industrial Engineering, School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology, Wuhan, China. I received a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering from College of Information Management, Nanjing Agricultural University, Nanjing, China, in 2019. In 2023, I was awarded a scholarship from the China Scholarship Council (CSC) to pursue study and research in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, under the supervision of Prof.[Andrea Matta](https://www.mecc.polimi.it/en/research/faculty/prof-andrea-matta). In 2025, I received dual Ph.D. degrees in Industrial Engineering from the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, Milan, Italy, and the Department of Industrial Engineering and Management at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China.

My research interests include
+ Analysis, modelling, and decision-making optimization for the sustainable operations management problems in manufacturing systems and service systems
+ Stochastic optimization
+ Exact optimization algorithm
+ Intelligent optimization algorithm
+ Reinforcement learning  
+ Statistical modeling analysis.
